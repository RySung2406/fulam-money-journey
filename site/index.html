YOUR PRIVACY

Your information, explained

The Fulin activity team runs this website. We use your HKU email to create an account and identify your own activity records.

  • We currently store account details, email verification status, and a generated participant ID.
  • Supabase processes account and email information. Brevo sends verification emails.
  • Your email is not displayed publicly. Activity analysis should use participant IDs.
  • Email verification confirms access to your mailbox, not your current student status.
  • Contact the activity team if you want to delete your account.

We do not access your inbox or ask for your university password. Sign out after using a shared device.
